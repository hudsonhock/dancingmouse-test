Devlog#2

Discovery of the creatures

The first creature made me believe there were more.


I found two more in a row, and three more in the next day. Six creatures in two days.

More followed in the days after, then I hit a wall. By the 12th creature, no matter how I modified the equations, nothing new came. Everything felt like something I had already seen. I thought: this is it. The space is exhausted. I am exhausted. Feeling a bit desperate because I couldn't see more new creatures coming out from this space.


I can't recall exactly what change in my mind, but something shifted, and new creatures started appearing again. So I was wrong. The space was never exhausted. I just hadn't found the next door yet. I am not a mathematician. I never was. What got me through was not formula knowledge â it was creativity in how I approached the modification, and lot of patience. That turned out to be enough.


Then I remembered an early conversation when I had just 14 creatures â we were imagining out loud: 20 feels reachable, 25 is hard, 30 is unimaginable, 50 is impossible. Once I surpass 50 I made a stop at 53. And I am still not sure the space has a ceiling. But I have to stop discovering, and start building an accessible bridge to release these creatures.
