DancingMouse is a game where you witness creatures emerge, and something on the other side starts to speak back.

You were classified as a visitor by the system that knew nothing about you. But it watched from the moment you arrived. You came here because something felt unfinished, like a question you couldn't quite name.


The creatures were already there before you arrived, fifty-three of them, living inside equations that nobody thought to look inside. Each one has a form that only emerges when the cursor dances in the right way.


You will not find them by being clever. You will find them by being patient. By learning the language of this place slowly, you shall witness them all, and more.
